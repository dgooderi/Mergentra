# Code of Conduct

Mergentra's issues, discussions and pull requests are for people to help each other and improve the project. Everyone taking part is expected to be respectful and constructive.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to Git, the project, or English.
- Disagree with ideas, not people. Give specific, actionable feedback.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Stay on topic and keep personal data, credentials and private repository details out of public posts.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, threats, or doxxing.
- Spam, advertising, or repeated off-topic posts.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, lock threads, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through their GitHub profile ([@dgooderi](https://github.com/dgooderi)) rather than replying in public. Reports are treated confidentially.

For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

This code of conduct is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
